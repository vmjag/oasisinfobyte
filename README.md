# 📊 Retail Sales EDA with Power BI

## 🔍 Overview
This project presents an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of retail sales data using Microsoft Power BI.

---

## 🧾 About the Dataset

### 🏢 Context
Ray Kroc wanted to build a restaurant system that would be famous for providing food of consistently high quality and uniform methods of preparation. He wanted to serve burgers, buns, fries, and beverages that tasted just the same in Alaska as they did in Alabama.

To achieve this, he chose a unique path: persuading both franchisees and suppliers to buy into his vision, working not for McDonald’s but for themselves, together with McDonald’s. Many of McDonald’s most famous menu items – like the Big Mac, Filet-O-Fish, and Egg McMuffin – were created by franchisees.

### 📦 Content
This dataset provides a nutritional analysis of every menu item on the US McDonald's menu, including:
- Breakfast
- Beef burgers
- Chicken and fish sandwiches
- Fries
- Salads
- Soda
- Coffee and tea
- Milkshakes
- Desserts

## 📁 Files Included

## 📈 Key Insights
- Trends in nutritional value across food categories
- Caloric distribution by product type
- Identification of high-sodium and high-sugar items
- Category-wise breakdown for better product positioning
